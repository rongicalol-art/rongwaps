## Ground truth

- **The printed book is the only source of truth.** Book 1 page scans are in `materials/book1/LXX/p<printed page>.png` (gitignored). Each lesson's transcript, `materials/book1/LXX/transcript.md`, is typed from those scans; every authored item must trace back to a line in it.

## Content rules (strict; the owner's law)

**Scope — nothing the learner hasn't been taught**
1. **Words:** only Book 1 vocabulary up to this lesson *part* (B1L01-2-xx words are Part 2: not allowed in a Part 1 exercise), plus words in the book's own grammar examples so far. Names and words the book itself prints in that exercise go in `allowWords`. A word the book prints beside the exercise goes in `allowWords`; nothing else does. `tests/content/grammarPracticeVocabulary.test.ts` enforces it.
2. **Grammar:** only grammar points taught *before or at this point, in book order*. An exercise for L1 point 3 may not use L1 point 4 (呢). Patterns from a later part of the same lesson count as untaught. The scope list (`.audit/grammar-run/scope.md`) shows what's taught and what's not yet. Watch the patterns made of known words: progressive 在 (L9), completed-action 了 (L11), 是…的 (L11), 先…再 (L9), comparisons (L9+), 就 (L10+).
3. **Book text only on pages:** tables and examples exactly as printed in the transcript. No invented example sentences anywhere on a page (clarifiers and labs may only re-show book sentences).

**Simplicity — make learners learn, no clutter**
4. **One decision per item:** the book's sentence stays whole, and the learner places only the grammar words (因為 / 所以 into their gaps, 吧 at the end, 好/難 + verb, 快要 … 了). Never make learners build clauses.
5. **Short tiles:** at most 4 characters per tile and at most 6 tiles per dock (the validator enforces both). Prefer 3–5 tiles.
6. **At least one tempting wrong tile** that targets the pattern's typical mistake, with a one-sentence repair. It must really be wrong in context; if a "wrong" tile could also be right, accept it or drop it.
7. **Every right answer has its sentence:** when several fills are right, each needs its own `result`/`alternatives` sentence (the validator enforces it).
8. **Fit the format to the book item; never force a template.** For each exercise, ask what the learner must notice (where a connector goes, a guess vs a question, now vs soon) and pick the kind that shows exactly that with the least clutter: `slot-build` (place words into the book sentence, optionally with a scene; with `gaps: true` the learner taps WHERE one word goes: every slot is a gap between words, only one lists the word, and each wrong gap has a `slot` repair saying why — use it when the book tests word position, e.g. 嗎/呢/也/都/了), `chat-build` (the book says "rearrange"), or `slot-reels` (the book says "write your own"). Vary across a lesson only when the book item differs. If no existing kind fits well, do not force one: leave that exercise out and write a `NEEDS-ENGINE:` line in `docs/grammar/reviews/LXX-needs.md` describing the interaction you would want (what the learner sees, taps and gets back). The design lead builds it.

**Faithfulness**
9. Follow the book's exercise: its instruction (`title`), its items in order, its pictures (`scene`: route / change / countdown). Don't swap an item for a "better" one. An open blank gets the most natural completion built from taught words, plus the accepted equivalents.
10. Keep the book's printed English. If it misleads, flag it as BOOK-ERRATA in the review rather than rewriting it.
11. Pinyin uses the book's tones, 一/不 sandhi, and neutral reduplication (看看 kàn kan).
14. **Learner-facing text only:** `title` is the book's exercise instruction in English (e.g. "Complete the sentences."), never "Exercise". `prompt`/`sub` speak to the learner ("Use 叫 or 姓.") or are omitted; never authoring notes like "The verb is supplied."
12. Repair notes are one plain-English sentence that names the fix. Explanations are short and paraphrase the book's note. Pattern labels follow `docs/GRAMMAR_LESSON_TEMPLATE.md`.
13a. **Never merge tables:** when the book prints several small tables (e.g. 月/號, 星期, 點/分), keep each as its own subsection with its own columns. Every printed cell (units like 號 and 分 included) appears, and no row has an empty slot that the book doesn't have.
13. Grammar pages mirror the book's layout: tables become `patternRows`/`subsections` (the book's (1)/(2)), and numbered sentences become `examples`. L8 (`content/grammar/lessonEight.json`) is the reference for page shape.

## Exercise design: be a good teacher (owner's standard, 2026-10-16)

Prototype to match: https://claude.ai/artifact/44QEgBfQtapzARTBjZe4hE

0. **Every book exercise item first, none dropped.** Each item in the book's 練習 boxes becomes one app item, in book order, with `source` naming it (e.g. "p.81 #3"); a coverage check fails on any missing one. Make each one smart: keep the book's sentence, and add the context (its dialogue line, its picture via the book-picture scene) that makes exactly one answer right. Only then add 1–3 of your own items per point, for a typical mistake the book items don't already test. Never drop a book item because it has a picture; describe the crop it needs in the review instead.
1. **Start from the mistake.** For each grammar point, name the 2–3 mistakes learners really make (the book's note plus common errors: 四星期 for 星期四, the day before the month, 嗎 after a question word, 很 before a noun). Every item tests exactly one of them.
2. **Exactly one right answer, decided by context.** The dialogue, sentence or picture makes one option right. If a second option could also be right, change the context or the option.
3. **Smart options:** 2–4 real, taught forms that are each right somewhere (嗎/呢, 叫/姓/是, 很/不是), so the learner has to think. Never a nonsense distractor.
4. **Three forms, picked per mistake:**
   - **Choose:** pick the one word for the gap.
   - **Order:** arrange 2–3 short tiles; only one order is right (星期四, 五月十六號, 九點二十分). Add one trap tile.
   - **Read:** a calendar, clock, route or before/now picture decides the answer.
4b. **No giveaways:** the answer must not be copyable from the context (B saying 她叫友美 gives away 叫, so B just says 友美。) or findable by elimination. All options look alike: same kind of word, similar length, all plausible here. If two options are obviously wrong, replace them.
5. **Every wrong option teaches:** its own one-sentence "why", naming what the context shows ("16 is under 四, not 三").
6. **3–5 items per grammar point**, mixing the forms. Later points in a lesson mix in earlier contrasts (嗎 vs 呢 once 呢 is taught).
7. **Build from the book:** its dialogues, names and words. Scope rules 1–2 apply strictly.
