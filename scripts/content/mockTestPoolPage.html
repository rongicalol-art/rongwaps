What a full paper needs

A Full test takes 50 listening and 50 reading questions, in the official paper's mix. "Needs" is one paper's share, "Has" is what the pool holds, and "Papers" is how many different full papers that fills before questions repeat. The aim is 2.

Questions per level

The Adaptive test draws from every level. It can only tell levels apart where questions exist, so an empty level is its ceiling.
