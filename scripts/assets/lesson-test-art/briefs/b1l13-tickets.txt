Kind: strip
Style: objects
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Each panel is a self-contained square scene. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same light wooden ticket counter seen from the customer's side, the same small ticket window frame behind it, and the same TICKET SELLER'S hand (adult, in a dark blue uniform sleeve) reaching out of the ticket window toward the viewer, passing paper TICKETS over the counter, fanned out like playing cards so each one can be counted. It must read clearly as the seller handing tickets to a buyer. Every ticket is a plain pale-yellow rectangle with a simple perforated stub edge and a small star shape, all identical. ONLY THE NUMBER OF TICKETS changes:
- Panel 1 (left): TWO tickets.
- Panel 2 (middle): THREE tickets.
- Panel 3 (right): FOUR tickets.
The tickets are large, clearly separated and do not overlap so much that one could be missed. Same hand, same counter, same window in all three panels. No other people, no text, no numbers, no prices on the tickets.
