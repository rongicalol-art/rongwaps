SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Each panel is a self-contained square scene. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: a street seen straight on. On the LEFT half of each panel stands the same tall, smart hotel building: glass revolving door, a red carpet at the entrance, a bellhop's brass luggage cart beside the door, rows of identical windows above. On the RIGHT half, directly next to the hotel, is a small one-storey shop; ONLY THIS SHOP changes:
- Panel 1 (left): POST OFFICE. A green shop front with a large white envelope symbol on its sign board (a symbol, no letters), and two Taiwanese street mailboxes in front of it (one green, one red).
- Panel 2 (middle): SUPERMARKET. A wide glass shop front with shelves of fruit and food visible inside, a row of red shopping carts and a stack of shopping baskets outside the door.
- Panel 3 (right): CAFÉ. A warm wooden shop front with a big coffee-cup symbol on its sign board, and two small round outdoor tables with coffee cups on them.
The hotel, the street and the sky are identical in all three panels. No people needed. No text anywhere: every sign uses a picture symbol only.
