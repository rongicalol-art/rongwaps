Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: a sunny street with TWO IDENTICAL small drink shops side by side (same size, same color, same awning, a simple drink-cup picture on each blank sign board, no writing), seen straight on. In front of each shop stands a loose crowd of customers (young people and adults, waiting for drinks; a loose group, not a neat line). ONLY THE SIZE OF THE TWO CROWDS changes, and the difference must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): a short queue at each shop, but the RIGHT shop's queue is clearly longer: 3 people queue at the left shop, 6 at the right shop.
- Panel 2 (middle): the LEFT shop's queue is clearly longer: 6 people queue at the left shop, 3 at the right shop.
- Panel 3 (right): both queues are EXACTLY the same length: 4 people at each shop.
Same street, shops and sky in all three panels. No text, no letters, no logos anywhere.
PHYSICAL CHECK: everything must be physically possible and natural. Each queue is ONE single-file line of complete people standing one behind another in front of its own counter, with a small clear gap between every person, so each one can be counted; no overlapping, merged or half-hidden people, no animal faces or blobs.
