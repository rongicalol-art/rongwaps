SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). The characters are the teacher, an adult woman (attached portrait: chin-length black bob, blue cardigan over a cream blouse, small green pendant) and one young male student (short black hair, plain grey T-shirt); keep both identical in all three panels. The teacher must read clearly as an adult and the student as a young man.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same small square wooden desk in the same quiet classroom corner, seen in a THREE-QUARTER FRONT VIEW. The student sits on a chair at the FAR side of the desk, facing the viewer, so the desk is between him and the viewer; he writes in an open notebook with a pencil, in the same pose in every panel. Only WHERE THE TEACHER IS changes:
- Panel 1 (left): ACROSS. The teacher sits on a chair at the NEAR side of the desk, facing the student, her back partly to the viewer (her face shown in a three-quarter view from behind), the desk between them, watching him write; her hands rest on the desk and she does not point.
- Panel 2 (middle): BESIDE. The teacher sits on a chair right next to the student, at his left (the viewer's right), on the same far side of the desk, both facing the viewer side by side, watching him write; her hands rest in her lap and she does not point.
- Panel 3 (right): BEHIND. The teacher STANDS squarely and directly BEHIND the student's chair, centered behind it, farther from the viewer than he is and taller than him, with BOTH hands resting on the top of the chair back, looking down over the top of his head at his notebook. No part of her is at his side: her head is above and behind his head, never beside it, and her arms do not reach past his shoulders.
The difference in her position must be obvious at a glance. No text anywhere (no readable writing in the notebook).
ART CHECK: panel 2 must be the ONLY panel where the teacher is at the student's side (旁邊 is tested on this strip). In ALL THREE panels she does the same thing: she only watches him write (every sentence says 看他寫字), so only her position differs. Reject any render where she points, holds a pen, or touches the notebook in any panel, or where, in panel 3, she leans over his shoulder or her head is level with or beside his.
