Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). The characters are an OLDER BROTHER, a teenage BOY about fifteen (short black hair, a red T-shirt, jeans), and his LITTLE SISTER, a GIRL about eight (two pigtails, a yellow dress), plus an ice-cream SELLER (a cheerful middle-aged man in a white cap and white apron) behind a small street cart. The boy must be clearly taller and older than the girl. Keep all three people's faces, hair and clothes identical in every panel.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same small ice-cream cart in a sunny park (a striped umbrella over the cart, a tree behind), seen from the side so the seller, the boy and the girl are all fully visible. In every panel ONE child pays and the seller hands ONE item to ONE child; WHO PAYS, WHO RECEIVES AND WHAT IS HANDED OVER must be unmistakable:
- Panel 1 (left): the BOY holds out coins to the seller with one hand. The seller leans over the cart and hands ONE ice-cream cone to the GIRL, who reaches up for it with a big smile. The boy has no ice cream.
- Panel 2 (middle): the GIRL holds out coins to the seller. The seller hands ONE ice-cream cone to the BOY, who takes it smiling. The girl has no ice cream.
- Panel 3 (right): the BOY holds out coins to the seller, exactly as in panel 1. The seller hands ONE small bottle of orange JUICE (clearly a drink bottle, not a cone) to the GIRL. Nobody has ice cream in this panel.
Only coins and one cone or one bottle change hands. No phones, no wallets with writing, no text anywhere (no signs, no price labels, no writing on the cart or the umbrella).
