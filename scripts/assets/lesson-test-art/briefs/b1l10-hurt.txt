SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The character is a YOUNG MAN, a university student (neat short black hair with a side part, plain ORANGE T-shirt, no hoodie, no jacket, nothing green), looking unwell with a pained face; keep him identical in all three panels. - Panel 2 (middle): BELLY: he holds his belly with BOTH hands, leaning forward a little (a stomachache); his hands are nowhere near his head.
- Panel 3 (right): EAR: he cups ONE hand over ONE ear, head tilted toward that side, wincing (an earache); his forehead and his other ear are clearly free, and the other hand rests on his knee.
