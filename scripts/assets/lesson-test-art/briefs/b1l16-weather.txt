Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). No people: a quiet mountain path in Taiwan's high mountains.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same narrow mountain path curving toward the back, the same three pine trees on the left, the same simple wooden bench on the right, the same mountain peaks behind. ONLY THE WEATHER changes, and it must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): SNOW. Big white snowflakes fall STRAIGHT down; a white layer of snow covers the path, the bench and the pine branches; the trees stand perfectly still (no wind). Soft grey-white sky.
- Panel 2 (middle): RAIN. Heavy grey rain streaks fall straight down; puddles on the path; NO snow anywhere; the trees stand still (no wind). Dark grey sky.
- Panel 3 (right): WIND. A strong wind bends the three pine trees hard to one side and blows leaves and a few twigs across the dry path (clear wind-swirl lines); NO rain and NO snow; patchy clouds in a pale sky.
Same path, trees, bench and peaks in all three panels. No people, no animals, no text anywhere.
