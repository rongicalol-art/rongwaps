SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). Two children, an older BROTHER (a boy of about 12, short black hair, blue T-shirt) and a younger SISTER (a girl of about 9, two pigtails, pink T-shirt); both wear dark shorts and sneakers. The learner tells them apart as the boy and the girl, so both must read clearly as a boy and a girl. Keep both identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same red running track seen from the side, the same green grass and trees behind; both children run from left to right. Only who is ahead changes:
- Panel 1 (left): the GIRL is clearly ahead, running FAST (leaning forward, long stride, speed lines, pigtails flying); the BOY is well behind her, jogging slowly and upright, a little tired, no speed lines.
- Panel 2 (middle): the BOY is clearly ahead, running FAST (leaning forward, long stride, speed lines); the GIRL is well behind him, jogging slowly and upright, no speed lines.
- Panel 3 (right): the BOY and the GIRL run SIDE BY SIDE, level with each other, BOTH running FAST: both leaning forward, long strides, speed lines behind both, hair flying.
The runner in front never slows down or looks back.
No finish line, no numbers, no other people. No text anywhere.
