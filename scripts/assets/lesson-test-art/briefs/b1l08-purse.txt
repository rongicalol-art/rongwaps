SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Each panel is a self-contained square scene. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same small round white table seen from a slight high angle, the same soft coral wall behind. ONE woman's handbag (purse) with a short handle stands in the middle of the table; it has the same shape and size in every panel. Only its COLOR or its CONDITION changes:
- Panel 1 (left): a NEW BLACK purse: shiny, clean, crisp edges, with a little sparkle highlight.
- Panel 2 (middle): an OLD BLACK purse: the same deep BLACK purse, still clearly black (do not fade it to grey); show its age only with scuffed and frayed corners, creases, a small patch and a loose thread on the handle.
- Panel 3 (right): a NEW YELLOW purse: shiny, clean, crisp edges, bright yellow, with a little sparkle highlight.
The purse is large and fills the middle of each panel so the difference is obvious at a glance. Exactly one purse in every panel. No people, no text, no logos.
