SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). The character is a LITTLE BOY about seven years old (short black hair, red T-shirt); keep him identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the little boy stands on a small step stool at the same white bathroom sink with the same round mirror and the same running tap, seen from the side so his face and hands are both visible. ONLY WHAT HE IS WASHING changes, and it must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): his HANDS: he rubs his soapy hands together under the running tap, white soap bubbles on his hands and fingers; his face and hair are completely dry.
- Panel 2 (middle): his FACE: he bends over the sink splashing water onto his face with cupped hands, water drops flying from his face; his hair is dry and has no foam.
- Panel 3 (right): his HAIR: his head is covered in a big white cap of shampoo foam and he rubs it with both hands; his face is dry.
