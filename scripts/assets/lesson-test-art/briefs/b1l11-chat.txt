Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The main character is a young Taiwanese university student, a YOUNG WOMAN (shoulder-length straight black hair held back with a yellow hair band, a plain light-green sweater); keep her identical in all three panels. She must not look like the book's Yiwen or Youmei (no high ponytail, no teal jacket, no bob with bangs, no red cardigan).
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the young woman sits on the same chair at the same wooden desk in the same small bedroom (a window with daylight behind the desk, a small plant on the desk, a laptop on the desk), seen from the waist up, smiling. ONLY HOW SHE IS CHATTING changes, and it must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): ON THE PHONE: she holds a mobile phone to her ear and talks into it, smiling. The laptop on the desk is CLOSED. Nobody else is in the room.
- Panel 2 (middle): ONLINE: she types on the OPEN laptop; the screen shows a chat window with a few plain coloured speech bubbles (no letters, no characters). Her phone lies face down on the desk. Nobody else is in the room.
- Panel 3 (right): FACE TO FACE: a second young woman (short curly brown hair, orange T-shirt) sits on a chair right next to her at the desk; the two face each other and talk and laugh. The laptop is CLOSED and the phone lies face down on the desk.
Same desk, same window, same plant, same clothes and hair in all three panels. No text anywhere.
