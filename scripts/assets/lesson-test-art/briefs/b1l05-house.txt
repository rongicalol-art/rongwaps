Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same small two-storey house with a red roof, a blue front door and two windows, seen from a slight three-quarter angle, with the same strip of green grass in front of it, a low white fence, two leafy trees beside it and neighbouring houses as a few simple shapes behind, under a deep cobalt-blue sky in warm late-afternoon sun with sparse hard-edged shadow shapes; a dominant cobalt and coral pair like the benchmark, not a yellow sky. On the grass IN FRONT OF the house sit exactly TWO animals, side by side, large and easy to count. Only the animals change:
- Panel 1 (left): TWO DOGS (brown and white).
- Panel 2 (middle): TWO CATS (orange and white).
- Panel 3 (right): ONE CAT (orange and white) and ONE DOG (brown and white).
Draw the animals in the same flat geometric illustration system as the benchmark people: simple rounded geometric shapes, small calm faces with dot eyes and a single-line mouth, no tongues, no collars, no big cartoon mascot heads; adult proportions for a dog and a cat. The dogs look clearly like dogs (floppy ears, longer snouts) and the cats clearly like cats (pointed ears, a few whisker lines). No other animals, no people, no text, no house number.

FROM REVIEW (overrides the base drawing above where they differ): Warm low late-afternoon sun from the left: long hard-edged shadow shapes of the house, the trees and the fence fall across the grass, one wall lit and one in shade, coral and yellow accents against the cobalt sky. The dogs and cats have adult proportions (head about a quarter of body height): the dogs with longer snouts, the cats with narrow faces, dot eyes and a single-line mouth, simple flat geometric shapes, never cute puppy or kitten mascots, no whisker-smiles. The same house, fence, trees and animal placement in all three panels. All light and shadow are flat hard-edged shapes: no gradients, no glow, no blur.
