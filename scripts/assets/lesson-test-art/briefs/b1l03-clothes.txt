SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The characters are Yiwen, a young woman (attached portrait: high dark ponytail with a coral tie, expressive eyes) on the LEFT and Zhongming, a young man (attached portrait: curly black hair) on the RIGHT; keep their faces and hair identical in all three panels. The learner tells them apart as the girl and the boy, so both must read clearly as a young woman and a young man.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: Yiwen (left) and Zhongming (right) standing side by side, seen from the knees up, smiling, on the same simple campus path with the same tree behind. Both wear the same simple long-sleeved top (no jacket) and plain dark navy trousers in every panel; ONLY THE COLOR of the tops changes, and it must be a clear, strong red or a clean white:
- Panel 1 (left): Yiwen's top WHITE, Zhongming's top RED.
- Panel 2 (middle): BOTH tops RED (Yiwen red, Zhongming red).
- Panel 3 (right): Yiwen's top RED, Zhongming's top WHITE.
The dark navy trousers, shoes, poses and background are identical in all three panels; nothing else they wear is red or white. No text anywhere.
