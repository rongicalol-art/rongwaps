SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Each panel is a self-contained square scene. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same light wooden desk top seen from a slight high angle, the same soft sage wall behind. On the LEFT of the desk lies ONE closed hardcover BOOK (plain teal cover, no writing); on the RIGHT stands ONE plain white CUP (a mug with no pattern), with a clear gap of empty desk between them. There is exactly ONE Chinese WRITING BRUSH (bamboo handle, black tip); only ITS POSITION changes:
- Panel 1 (left): the brush lies flat on the desk in the empty space BETWEEN the book and the cup, touching neither of them. The cup is empty.
- Panel 2 (middle): the brush lies flat ON TOP OF the book. The cup is empty.
- Panel 3 (right): the brush stands upright INSIDE the cup, its handle sticking out of the top. Nothing on the book.
Same book, same cup, same positions in all three panels; the brush is large enough to see at a glance. No people, no text.
