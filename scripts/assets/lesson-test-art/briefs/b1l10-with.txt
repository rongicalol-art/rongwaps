SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The main character is a young Taiwanese university student, a YOUNG WOMAN (dark hair in a HIGH PONYTAIL, light PINK sweater, jeans, a small backpack, no scarf); keep her identical in all three panels. She must not look like the book's Yuanzhen (no shoulder-length loose hair, no blue top, no red scarf).
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the young woman stands on the LEFT side of each panel, seen from the knees up, in front of the same glass entrance door of a small hospital (a plain red cross symbol on the door, a potted plant beside it). Next to her, on the RIGHT, stands ONE family member; ONLY WHO stands next to her changes, and each must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): her MOTHER, a middle-aged woman (short permed hair, cardigan), standing beside her.
- Panel 2 (middle): her FATHER, a middle-aged man (short grey-streaked hair, collared shirt), standing beside her.
- Panel 3 (right): her LITTLE BROTHER, a small boy about seven years old (much shorter than her, T-shirt and shorts), standing beside her.
Same door, same plant, same pose and clothes for the young woman in all three panels. Exactly two people in every panel. No text anywhere (the red cross is a plain symbol, no letters).
