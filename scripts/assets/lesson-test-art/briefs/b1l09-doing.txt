Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The character is one young woman student (simple, friendly, shoulder-length dark hair, a plain mustard sweater); keep her face, hair and clothes identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: she sits at the same small wooden desk in a cozy dorm room, seen from the side and slightly above so the desk top is clearly visible, the same bunk bed and window behind her, the same desk lamp. Only WHAT SHE IS DOING at the desk changes, and the tool in her hand must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): CALLIGRAPHY. She holds a long Chinese WRITING BRUSH upright and paints a big black brush stroke on a large sheet of plain white paper; a small black ink dish sits beside the paper. (The stroke is an abstract swoosh, not a readable character.)
- Panel 2 (middle): HOMEWORK. She writes in an open lined notebook with a short yellow PENCIL; the large white paper and the brush are not there.
- Panel 3 (right): READING. She holds an open book in both hands and reads it; nothing in her hands but the book, no pencil and no brush on the desk.
Same desk, same room, same lamp in all three panels. DAYTIME in all three panels: bright daylight in the window and the desk lamp switched OFF. No text anywhere (no readable writing on the paper, notebook or book).
