Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). The characters are one young man student (short black hair, plain grey sweater) sitting on the LEFT and one young woman student (short ponytail, plain yellow top) sitting on the RIGHT; they are generic students, not any of the book's cast, so do not give them a green hoodie or a coral cardigan. Keep their faces and clothes identical in all three panels. The learner tells them apart as the boy and the girl, so both must read clearly as a young man and a young woman.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the boy (left) and the girl (right) sit side by side at the same long library table, seen from the front, waist up, with the same bookshelves behind them. In front of each of them on the table lies their own pile of books; only THE NUMBER OF BOOKS in each pile changes:
- Panel 1 (left): in front of the BOY a TALL stack of SIX thick books; in front of the GIRL only ONE book.
- Panel 2 (middle): in front of the BOY only ONE book; in front of the GIRL a TALL stack of SIX thick books.
- Panel 3 (right): in front of the BOY ONE book and in front of the GIRL ONE book (equal).
The books have plain colored covers with no writing. The tall stacks must be clearly taller than the single books. Same poses, same table, same shelves in all three panels. No text anywhere.
