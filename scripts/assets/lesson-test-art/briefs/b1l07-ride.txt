SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The character is one young woman, a university student (shoulder-length brown hair, mustard-yellow sweater, dark jeans, small backpack); keep her identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same city sidewalk and curb seen from the side, the same row of shop fronts and the same street tree behind (no signs, no writing). The young woman is seen full-body in the middle of each panel. Only HOW SHE TRAVELS changes:
- Panel 1 (left): WALKING. She walks along the sidewalk mid-stride, arms swinging. The curb next to her is EMPTY: no bus, no taxi, no car anywhere.
- Panel 2 (middle): BUS. A big city bus (teal and white) stands at the curb with its front door open; she is stepping up into the door, one foot on the bus step.
- Panel 3 (right): TAXI. A small yellow Taiwanese taxi (sedan) stands at the curb with its back door open; she is bending to get into the back seat.
The vehicle must be large and unmistakable (a long bus with many windows versus a small yellow car). Nothing else changes. No text anywhere: no route numbers, no signs, no plates with writing.
PHYSICAL CHECK: everything must be physically possible and natural. In the taxi panel the rear door is swung OPEN on its hinges (the door panel with its window visibly sticking out), and she steps into the open doorway.
