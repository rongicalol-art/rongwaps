SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The character is one young man, a university student (short black hair, green hoodie, grey backpack strap on one shoulder); keep him identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the young man seen from the waist up, standing on the same sunny Taipei street corner with the same lamp post, the same leafy tree and small shop fronts as a few simple shapes behind him, bright daylight with sparse hard-edged shadow shapes. He is an unnamed student drawn in exactly the benchmark's illustration system (geometric proportions, simplified face, single-line mouth); calm and focused, not frowning. Only WHAT HE IS LOOKING AT in his hands changes:
- Panel 1 (left): PAPER MAP. He holds a big unfolded paper street map open with both hands and looks down at it (simple map shapes: streets, green park blocks, a blue river; no writing). No phone in his hands or pocket; his left sleeve covers his wrist, so no watch shows.
- Panel 2 (middle): PHONE. He holds a smartphone up in one hand at chest height and looks down at its screen. The screen faces HIM, tilted toward his face; we see it from a low side angle, enough to tell it shows a map (streets and a location pin, no writing). Never turn the screen toward the viewer while he looks at it. No paper map anywhere; his left sleeve covers his wrist, so no watch shows.
- Panel 3 (right): WATCH. He raises his left wrist and looks at his wristwatch (a round analogue watch face, clearly visible, large enough to read as a watch; the hands show 3:40). His hands hold no phone and no map.
Each object is large and obvious at thumbnail size. Nothing else changes. No text anywhere.
