Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). The character is a BOY, a university student (short black hair, green T-shirt, a backpack on one shoulder); keep him identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing, framed TIGHTLY on the classroom doorway and the wall beside it (no desks or only a cut-off desk edge, so no panel shows an empty or a full class): the boy is stepping in through the open door; on the wall right beside the door, at his head height or a little above, hangs a LARGE, simple round wall clock with a white face and the Arabic numerals 1 to 12 printed large and clear around the edge (the 12 at the top), and two clear black hands: a SHORT, THICK hour hand and a LONG, THIN minute hand. The clock must be big enough to read at phone size. ONLY THE TIME ON THE CLOCK changes:
- Panel 1 (left): HALF PAST SEVEN (7:30): the minute hand points straight DOWN at the 6; the thick hour hand points clearly halfway between the 7 and the 8.
- Panel 2 (middle): EIGHT O'CLOCK (8:00): the minute hand points straight UP at the 12; the thick hour hand points exactly at the 8.
- Panel 3 (right): HALF PAST EIGHT (8:30): the minute hand points straight DOWN at the 6; the thick hour hand points clearly halfway between the 8 and the 9.
Same boy, same pose, same doorway and same light in all three panels; no other people. The numerals 1–12 on the clock face are the ONLY writing: no other text or numbers anywhere (not on the board, the door or the walls).
