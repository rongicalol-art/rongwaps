SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The character is a young Taiwanese university student, a YOUNG WOMAN (a short pixie cut of dark hair with a small yellow hair clip, plain light-green sweater); keep her identical in all three panels. She must not look like the book's Yiwen, Youmei or Yuanzhen (no high ponytail, no teal jacket, no bangs bob, no shoulder-length straight bob, no blue top, no red scarf).
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the young woman, seen from the waist up in the same simple room with a plain cream wall, smiles and holds up ONE white greeting card with both hands, facing the viewer, so its front fills a large part of the panel. The front of the card shows ONE big, simple, friendly cartoon animal; ONLY THE ANIMAL ON THE CARD changes, and each must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): a brown MONKEY with a long curly tail.
- Panel 2 (middle): a green SNAKE, long and curvy.
- Panel 3 (right): a round pink PIG.
Same woman, same pose, same card size, same room in all three panels; exactly one animal on each card and no other animals anywhere. No text anywhere (no writing on the card).
