SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Each panel is a self-contained square scene. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same warm wooden café table top seen from a slight high angle, the same small plant at the back left, the same soft teal wall behind. Only the food/drink on the table changes:
- Panel 1 (left): TWO matching white cups of black tea (deep red-brown tea, a little steam), side by side.
- Panel 2 (middle): TWO matching blue-and-white bowls of Taiwanese beef noodle soup (noodles, beef chunks, green bok choy, steam), side by side.
- Panel 3 (right): TWO matching clear glass bottles of orange juice, side by side.
The objects are large and fill the middle of each panel so the difference is obvious at a glance. Exactly two items in every panel. No people, no text, no price tags.
