SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). The scene is a small, quiet school LIBRARY room with no people in it.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing, seen straight on from inside the room: on the LEFT side of the back wall, one plain wooden DOOR; on the RIGHT side of the back wall, one large WINDOW with two glass panes that swing open; between them, a tall bookshelf full of books and a reading table with two chairs in front. ONLY WHETHER THE DOOR AND THE WINDOW ARE OPEN OR SHUT changes, and it must be obvious at a glance (an open door shows the bright hallway behind it; an open window has both panes swung wide with a light breeze moving a curtain; a shut door and a shut window are flat and closed):
- Panel 1 (left): the DOOR is wide OPEN; the WINDOW is SHUT.
- Panel 2 (middle): the DOOR is SHUT; the WINDOW is wide OPEN.
- Panel 3 (right): the DOOR and the WINDOW are BOTH wide OPEN.
Everything else (bookshelf, table, chairs, light) identical in all three panels. No people, no text anywhere (no signs, no writing on the books).

SETTING: wherever this brief says a plain wall, a bare table edge or a plain backdrop, make it a real lit place in the benchmark's style instead (a window or room edge, depth, hard-edged flat shapes of sunlight, a cast shadow), never a lone icon on a flat colour strip. The object, and any exact writing the brief gives, stay large, flat-on to the viewer and fully readable, and the place stays identical across panels.
