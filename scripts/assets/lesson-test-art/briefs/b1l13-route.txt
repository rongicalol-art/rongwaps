SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Each panel is a self-contained square scene. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: a hand holding a smartphone upright, the screen large and filling the middle of the panel. The screen shows the same simple flat street map seen from above: light grey streets on a pale green background. At the BOTTOM CENTER of the map is a small round MRT station symbol (a simple train-front icon in a blue circle, no letters) with a blue "you are here" dot beside it. One straight street runs from the station UP to ONE big CROSSROADS in the middle of the map (a plain four-way crossing with zebra stripes); from the crossroads streets go LEFT, RIGHT and STRAIGHT ON. ONLY THE ROUTE changes: a thick dotted RED route line starts at the blue dot, runs up to the crossroads and then:
- Panel 1 (left): turns LEFT at the crossroads and ends at a red map pin on the left street.
- Panel 2 (middle): goes STRAIGHT ACROSS the crossroads, with no turn, and ends at a red map pin on the street straight ahead, just past the crossroads.
- Panel 3 (right): turns RIGHT at the crossroads and ends at a red map pin on the right street.
Exactly one red pin and one route in every panel; the streets, the station symbol and the hand are identical in all three panels. No arrows other than the dotted line, no street names, no text, no numbers anywhere.

SETTING: wherever this brief says a plain wall, a bare table edge or a plain backdrop, make it a real lit place in the benchmark's style instead (a window or room edge, depth, hard-edged flat shapes of sunlight, a cast shadow), never a lone icon on a flat colour strip. The object, and any exact writing the brief gives, stay large, flat-on to the viewer and fully readable, and the place stays identical across panels.
