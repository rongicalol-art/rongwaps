Kind: strip

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?").
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: a simple flat OUTLINE MAP of the island of TAIWAN (its real long north–south leaf shape, north at the top), green land on pale blue sea, filling most of the panel height, with a soft line of mountains drawn down the middle. No other islands, no cities, no roads, no compass. ONLY THE POSITION OF ONE RED MAP PIN changes, and it must be obvious at a glance:
- Panel 1 (left): the red pin stands near the NORTHERN TIP of the island (top).
- Panel 2 (middle): the red pin stands in the MIDDLE of the island (half way between top and bottom).
- Panel 3 (right): the red pin stands in the SOUTHERN part of the island (near the bottom).
Exactly one pin per panel, the same size and color in all three. Same map in all three panels. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere.

SETTING: wherever this brief says a plain wall, a bare table edge or a plain backdrop, make it a real lit place in the benchmark's style instead (a window or room edge, depth, hard-edged flat shapes of sunlight, a cast shadow), never a lone icon on a flat colour strip. The object, and any exact writing the brief gives, stay large, flat-on to the viewer and fully readable, and the place stays identical across panels.
