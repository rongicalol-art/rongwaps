SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The character is one young man (about 20, short black hair, plain white T-shirt, blue jeans); keep his face, hair and clothes identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the young man stands facing us, full body, smiling, right next to the same plain wooden door of a simple living room (cream wall, the same small rug on the floor). The door is the height reference and is exactly the same size and place in every panel. Only his HEIGHT and his BUILD change:
- Panel 1 (left): TALL and THIN. The top of his head almost reaches the top of the door frame; he is slim.
- Panel 2 (middle): SHORT and THIN. He is still the same ADULT, not a child: keep his adult face, adult body proportions and the same clothes; he is only shorter. The top of his head is at about three quarters of the door's height, clearly lower than in panels 1 and 3; he is slim.
- Panel 3 (right): TALL and FAT. The top of his head almost reaches the top of the door frame, exactly as in panel 1; his body is clearly round and heavy (a friendly, non-mocking drawing).
The door, room, clothes colors and pose are identical in all three panels. No text anywhere.
