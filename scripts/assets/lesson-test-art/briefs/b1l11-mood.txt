SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a listening question ("which picture matches what you hear?"). The character is a YOUNG MAN, a university student (short neat black hair, round black glasses, a plain grey hoodie); keep him identical in all three panels. He must not look like the book's Zhongming or Guo'an (no curly hair, no yellow overshirt, no spiky hair, no green hoodie).
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the young man sits on the same chair at the same desk in the same plain room (a lamp and a closed notebook on the desk, a window behind), seen from the waist up, facing the viewer. ONLY HIS MOOD changes, shown by his face and body, and each must be unmistakable:
- Panel 1 (left): HAPPY: a big open smile, eyes bright, both arms raised in joy.
- Panel 2 (middle): NERVOUS: he sits up stiffly, eyes wide, mouth a tight wavy line, one big drop of sweat on his forehead, both hands gripping his knees.
- Panel 3 (right): SAD: he slumps forward with his head down, shoulders dropped, eyes looking down, a downturned mouth; no tears.
No other symbols (no hearts, no rain clouds); keep the single sweat drop in panel 2. No other people, no text.
