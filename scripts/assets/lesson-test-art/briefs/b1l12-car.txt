Kind: strip
Style: scene
Characters: none

SUBJECT: a 3-panel picture strip for a reading question ("which picture matches the sentence?"). The characters are a FATHER (a man about forty, short black hair, glasses, a plain grey shirt) driving a car, and his SON, a little BOY about seven years old (short black hair, a red T-shirt), in the back seat. Keep both identical in all three panels.
LAYOUT: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536x1024). Three EQUAL square panels side by side in one horizontal row, vertically centered, separated by wide plain WHITE gutters (at least 40px) and a plain white margin around all of them. Do not number or label the panels.
ALL THREE PANELS share the same base drawing: the same small family car seen from the side, cut away and framed CLOSE: the BOY in the back seat fills about HALF of each panel; in front only the father's head, shoulders, both hands and the steering wheel show, eyes on the road; the boy sits in the back seat with his seat belt on and a small pillow against the window beside him; the same sunny street outside the windows. The father looks the same in every panel. ONLY WHAT THE BOY IS DOING changes:
- Panel 1 (left): SLEEPING: the boy is fast asleep: head slumped sideways onto the small pillow against the window, body relaxed, eyes clearly closed, mouth closed, hands resting in his lap; no music notes and no food.
- Panel 2 (middle): SINGING: the boy sits up and sings happily, mouth wide open, a few small music notes floating near his mouth; his eyes are open; his hands rest in his lap; no bread or food anywhere.
- Panel 3 (right): EATING BREAD: the boy sits up and eats a BIG bread roll held in both hands, eyes open, cheeks full; no music notes.
Exactly two people in every panel. No text anywhere.
