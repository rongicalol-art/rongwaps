### Lesson 15 — Dialogue 1 (對話一)
- **Audio**: `B1-15-1-1.mp3`
- **Characters**: 家樂 (Jialé), 友美 (Yǒuměi), 元真 (Yuánzhēn)

```text
家樂: 我們下個星期要學第十五課，我剛才先看了一點，書上有很多種動物，我覺得很有意思。
友美: 有哪些動物？
家樂: 我記得有羊、馬、兔子，最特別的是龍。你看看！
友美: 原來是十二生肖，真的很有意思。
家樂: 什麼是十二生肖？
友美: 華人用不一樣的動物代表每一年，一共有十二種動物。
元真: 越南也有十二生肖，可是我們沒有兔年，有貓年。我是貓年出生的，屬貓。
家樂: 真的？好有趣！原來越南的生肖跟中國的有點不一樣。我好想知道我屬什麼。
友美: 很簡單，上網一查就知道了。
家樂: 好，我上網查查看。
```

**English Translation**:
- Jialé: We are going to study Lesson 15 next week. I just glanced at it earlier. There are many kinds of animals in the book, which I think is very interesting.
- Yǒuměi: What animals are there?
- Jialé: I remember there are goats, horses, rabbits, and the most special one is the dragon. Take a look!
- Yǒuměi: Oh, so it’s the Chinese zodiac! That’s really fascinating.
- Jialé: What is the Chinese zodiac?
- Yǒuměi: Chinese people use different animals to represent each year. There are twelve animals in total.
- Yuánzhēn: Vietnam also has the twelve zodiac animals, but we don't have the Year of the Rabbit; we have the Year of the Cat. I was born in the Year of the Cat, so my zodiac sign is the cat.
- Jialé: Really? How interesting! It turns out Vietnam's zodiac is a bit different from China's. I really want to know what sign I am.
- Yǒuměi: It's very simple. Just look it up online and you'll know immediately.
- Jialé: Okay, I'll search for it online.

### Lesson 15 — Reading (短文): 有趣的十二生肖
- **Audio**: `B1-15-3-1.mp3`

```text
今天一進教室，就看到桌上放著幾隻用紙做的動物，黑板上也有很多張動物的圖片，我覺得很有趣。
老師告訴我們，很久以前，有十二種動物一起參加了一個過河比賽，看誰游得最快。
開始比賽以後，每隻動物都游得很快很辛苦。老鼠先過了河，第二個到的是牛，最後到的是豬。老鼠贏了，排第一，其他動物排後面。
這次比賽以後，華人就用鼠、牛、虎、兔、龍、蛇、馬、羊、猴、雞、狗和豬代表每一年，而且叫這十二種動物「十二生肖」。
華人聊天的時候，會問別人「你屬什麼？」。每年到了元宵節，代表那年的動物燈籠，也會最受歡迎。
```

**English Translation**:
As soon as I walked into the classroom today, I saw several paper animals placed on the table, and there were also many pictures of animals on the blackboard. I found it very interesting.
The teacher told us that a long time ago, twelve animals entered a river-crossing race together to see who could swim the fastest.
After the race began, each animal swam hard and fast. The rat crossed the river first, the ox arrived second, and the pig arrived last. The rat won, ranking first, and the other animals lined up behind.
After this race, Chinese people used the rat, ox, tiger, rabbit, dragon, snake, horse, goat, monkey, rooster, dog, and pig to represent each year, calling these twelve animals the "Twelve Zodiac Animals".
When chatting, Chinese people often ask others, "What is your zodiac sign?". Every year during the Lantern Festival, the lantern representing the animal of that year is also the most popular.

### Lesson 15 — Core Grammar Points (語法)
1. **Comparison with (不)一樣**:
   - Structure: `A 跟 B (不)一樣 (+ Vs)`
   - Examples:
     - 越南的生肖跟中國的有點不一樣。
     - 他的想法跟我不一樣。
2. **Durative Aspect Marker 著**:
   - Structure: `S + V1 + 著 + (O1) + V2 + O2` or `Location + V + 著 + Noun`
   - Examples:
     - 你手裡拿著什麼？
     - 桌上放著幾隻用紙做的動物。
     - 很多大人帶著小孩去看燈會。
3. **Immediate Sequence 一……就……**:
   - Structure: `S + 一 + V1 + 就 + V2`
   - Examples:
     - 很簡單，上網一查就知道了。
     - 我打算一下班就去看燈會。
     - 今天一進教室，就看到桌上放著動物。

### Lesson 16 — Dialogue 1 (對話一)
- **Audio**: `B1-16-1-1.mp3`
- **Characters**: 家樂 (Jialé), 國安 (Guó'ān)

```text
家樂: 國安，這次學校放假，你有什麼計畫？
國安: 我還沒打算要做什麼。這次放假，沒有上次放得那麼久，只放三天假，我不知道去哪裡比較好。
家樂: 我覺得太魯閣不錯，有山又有河。
國安: 在哪裡？離台北遠不遠？

國安: 那你打算去哪裡玩？

```

**English Translation**:
- Jialé: Guo'an, school is going on vacation this time. Do you have any plans?
- Guó'ān: I haven't decided what to do yet. This break is not as long as the last one—only three days—so I don't know where it's best to go.
- Jialé: I think Taroko Gorge is great; it has both mountains and rivers.
- Guó'ān: Where is it? Is it far from Taipei?
- Jialé: It's in Hualien. Hualien is in eastern Taiwan, somewhat far from Taipei. You can take a train or drive there; it takes about four hours.
- Guó'ān: Have you been there?
- Jialé: I went when I was in sixth grade of elementary school. I liked it very much.
- Guó'ān: Where do you plan to go for fun?
- Jialé: I want to go to Hehuanshan. The newspaper says it might snow these few days.
- Guó'ān: Snow? I haven't seen snow before. Where is Hehuanshan?
- Jialé: In central Taiwan. Sun Moon Lake is also nearby.
- Guó'ān: Sun Moon Lake is very famous. I read about it in a book. Is it close to Hehuanshan?
- Jialé: Not far. I want to go for three days and two nights: go to Sun Moon Lake first, and then to Hehuanshan.
- Guó'ān: Remember to take more pictures for me to see!

### Lesson 16 — Reading (短文): 介紹台灣
- **Audio**: `B1-16-3-1.mp3`

```text
台灣四邊都是海，西邊有中國大陸，南邊有菲律賓。夏天沒有菲律賓那麼熱，冬天沒有中國北部那麼冷。
台灣不大，比荷蘭小一點，從北到南只有385公里。但是有山有海，風景漂亮的地方很多。而且交通很方便，從山上到海邊，不需要太多時間，有的外國人覺得這樣很有意思。
台灣最有名的城市是台北。台北的捷運又方便又乾淨，搭捷運上班上學的人很多，來旅行的人也很喜歡搭台北的捷運。
每年有很多外國人到台灣來旅行。他們喜歡去夜市，因為那裡的食物跟飲料非常多，也很熱鬧，還可以玩很多好玩的遊戲。最有名的食物是小籠包、牛肉麵跟珍珠奶茶，台灣人喜歡，很多外國人也很喜歡。
```

**English Translation**:
Taiwan is surrounded by the sea on all four sides, with Mainland China to the west and the Philippines to the south. Summer is not as hot as the Philippines, and winter is not as cold as northern China.
Taiwan is not big—a bit smaller than the Netherlands, only 385 kilometers from north to south. However, it has both mountains and the sea, with many scenic spots. Furthermore, transportation is very convenient: going from the mountains to the coast doesn't take much time, which some foreigners find fascinating.
Taiwan's most famous city is Taipei. Taipei's MRT is both convenient and clean. Many people ride the MRT to work and school, and travelers also enjoy taking it.
Every year, many foreigners travel to Taiwan. They like visiting night markets because there is a wide variety of food and drinks, the atmosphere is lively, and they can play many fun games. The most famous foods are soup dumplings (xiaolongbao), beef noodles, and bubble tea—loved by Taiwanese and foreigners alike.

### Lesson 16 — Core Grammar Points (語法)
1. **Distance Marker 離**:
   - Structure: `A 離 B 遠 / 近`
   - Examples:
     - 離台北遠不遠？
     - 學校離我家很近。
2. **Shift in Circumstance: 本來……後來……**:
   - Structure: `S + 本來 + Clause 1，(可是/但是) + 後來 + Clause 2`
   - Examples:
     - 我本來要去那裡，可是因為沒有車票了，所以後來決定去高雄玩。
     - 他本來想學日文，後來決定學中文。
3. **Sequence with Completed Action: “S + V + 了 + O” 就 …**:
   - Structure: `S + V1 + 了 + O1，就 + V2 + O2`
   - Examples:
     - 下了火車就坐捷運去西子灣了。
     - 吃了飯就去圖書館。
4. **Comparative Degree 更**:
   - Structure: `更 + Adj / Vs`
   - Examples:
     - 你應該搭高鐵去，這樣更快。
     - 他的中文比以前更好了。
5. **State/Manner Complement with 得**:
   - Structure: `S + V + 得 + Complement (很開心 / 好開心 / 很高興)`
   - Examples:
     - 我玩得很開心。
     - 你笑得好開心。
