# Dialogue Scene Composition Prompts: Batch 1 (Lessons 1–4)

A curated collection of minimalist, geometric flat vector scene composition prompts for the textbook dialogues in Book 1 (Lessons 1 to 4, 8 dialogues total).

Each dialogue scene is designed in a **16:9 landscape banner format (2048×1143)** to serve as an engaging header visual above the interactive dialogue reader. 

### Consistency Architecture:
To eliminate character drift across generated images, all prompts use the **exact, immutable Master Character Visual DNA formula** (standardized hair geometry, skin tones, layered outfits, and signature color tokens).

# Lesson 1: 新同學 · The New Classmate

## Dialogue 1: 在教室 · In the classroom (B1L01-R01)
*Context: Zhongming spots new Japanese classmate Youmei across the room and leans over to ask Yiwen who she is.*

Scene Concept: Whispers Across the Classroom Aisle
Eye-level two-shot composition of Zhongming leaning across his wooden desk to question Yiwen, who turns with an amused, knowing smile.

Foreground & Center:
- [Focal Character/Object]: Zhongming (李中明)—energetic young male Indonesian student, warm golden-tan skin tone, voluminous textured wavy jet-black hair in soft rounded pill clusters, friendly dark eyes with an inquisitive expression; wearing an open mustard-yellow long-sleeve cotton overshirt over a solid cyan-blue crewneck t-shirt, relaxed straight-leg navy-blue trousers, and rounded white sneakers; leaning across his desk with one hand gesturing discreetly toward the background.
- [Secondary Character/Props]: Yiwen (王宜文)—energetic young female student, fair skin tone, high glossy jet-black ponytail sweeping upward in a dynamic tapered arc with a coral-pink elastic band, alert dark eyes with a knowing arc smile; wearing a zipped deep-teal athletic track jacket with a rounded stand-up collar and coral-pink zipper pull, and charcoal-gray slim joggers; seated at the adjacent desk turning to whisper back; on the twin desks sit an open lined notebook, a yellow hexagonal pencil, and a slim cylindrical aluminum water flask.

Background & Environment:
- The Space: A bright minimalist classroom with flat pale-wood floor planes, clean modular desks, and a dark slate-green chalkboard plane on the left.
- Framing & Backdrops: A large floor-to-ceiling rectangular window with a rounded arch top, through which the distant soft silhouette of Youmei (jet-black bob, coral cardigan) reading by a sunlit locker is visible.

Lighting & Visual Balance:
- Crisp morning sunlight streaming from the right window, illuminating Yiwen's ponytail arc and Zhongming's mustard-yellow shirt, leading the viewer's eye along his pointing gesture toward the background window.

Scene Concept: Placing the Noodle and Tea Order
Direct frontal eye-level view of Zhongming and Youmei at a wooden counter ordering two bowls of beef noodles and iced teas from the smiling cafe worker.

Foreground & Center:
- [Focal Character/Object]: Zhongming (李中明)—energetic young male Indonesian student, warm golden-tan skin tone, voluminous textured wavy jet-black hair in soft rounded pill clusters; wearing his open mustard-yellow long-sleeve overshirt over a solid cyan-blue crewneck t-shirt, relaxed navy trousers, and white sneakers; standing at the counter holding up two fingers to indicate two bowls while extending a payment bill with a friendly smile.
- [Secondary Character/Props]: The Cafe Worker (女店員)—cheerful young female shop assistant, warm fair skin tone, jet-black hair pulled back into a sleek spherical low bun with a side sweep, bright welcoming broad arc smile; wearing a vibrant sunshine-yellow rectangular bib apron with waist ties over a dark teal short-sleeved collared polo shirt; tapping on a modern flat tablet register; Youmei (小林友美) in her signature jet-black bob, coral-pink cardigan, ivory blouse, and charcoal skirt stands alongside holding a receipt slip; on the counter sit two large steaming ceramic soup bowls with chopsticks and two clear cups of iced tea with diagonal straws.
