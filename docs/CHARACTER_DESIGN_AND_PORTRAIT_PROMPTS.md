# Character Design & Profile Picture Prompts

A complete set of minimalist, geometric flat vector prompts for updating the RongWaps character cast. Contains both **Full-Body Base Design Sheet Prompts** and **Profile Picture (Avatar Portrait) Prompts** for all 12 canonical characters.

Each prompt strictly avoids dialogue and art style descriptors, translating each character purely into rounded geometry, signature color palettes, and clear spatial balance.

# Main Cast

## 1. Youmei (小林友美)
*Identity: 19-year-old student from Japan. Calm, observant newcomer. Signature dark bob, coral cardigan over white blouse.*

### A. Full-Body Base Design Sheet
Scene Concept: Youmei — Full-Body Character Model
Centered, straight-on full-length standing pose of Youmei showcasing her complete silhouette, signature outfit layers, and proportions.

Foreground & Center:
- [Focal Character/Object]: Youmei—a 19-year-old student with a pill-shaped torso, rounded geometric chin, and a glossy jet-black chin-length bob hair helmet with straight horizontal bangs; wearing an open coral-pink knit cardigan layered over a crisp ivory collared button-down blouse, a charcoal-gray pleated A-line skirt, and smooth rounded loafers with clean white ankle socks.
- [Secondary Character/Props]: A soft-cornered rectangular canvas tote bag slung over one shoulder with a circular enamel pin, a slim pastel notebook tucked under her arm, and an upright cylindrical travel water flask.

Background & Environment:
- The Space: A clean, neutral studio stage with a solid pale-cream floor plane and a subtle horizontal baseline cast shadow.
- Framing & Backdrops: A large, soft sage-green solid circle backdrop centered directly behind the character's torso and head to establish silhouette contrast.

Lighting & Visual Balance:
- High-key, even ambient lighting with soft downward illumination, placing warm coral and ivory tones at the visual center against cool sage-green framing.

---

### B. Profile Picture (Avatar Portrait)
Scene Concept: Youmei — Profile Avatar Portrait
Tight frontal bust portrait centered from the collarbone up, optimized for a circular application icon.

Foreground & Center:
- [Focal Character/Object]: Youmei's head and shoulders—featuring her signature dark chin-length bob with crisp horizontal bangs, subtle upturned arc smile, round dark eyes, and the visible upper lapels of her coral-pink cardigan over the clean pointed collar of her white blouse.
- [Secondary Character/Props]: A single small circular cherry-blossom enamel pin clipped to the cardigan lapel, and a thin diagonal strap of her tote bag crossing the shoulder.

Background & Environment:
- The Space: A seamless, flat vibrant mint-teal circular field serving as an uncluttered backdrop.
- Framing & Backdrops: A thin concentric white ring outline framing the outer boundary of the circular avatar zone.

Lighting & Visual Balance:
- Soft frontal rim lighting emphasizing the clean curve of the dark bob haircut against the contrasting bright mint-teal background.
