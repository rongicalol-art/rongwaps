# Reading Narrative Scene Composition Prompts

A complete set of minimalist, geometric flat vector scene composition prompts for the 16 reading narratives (短文 · Reading) in Book 1. Each prompt is stripped of dialogue and narrative prose, translating the scenario strictly into rounded geometry, clear spatial positioning, and 3–5 essential visual storytelling props. Where canonical book characters appear, their established visual identities (hair silhouettes, signature color palettes, and layered attire) are strictly woven into the geometric descriptions.

---

## Lesson 1: 自我介紹 · Self-Introduction (B1L01-R03)

Scene Concept: The Welcome Refreshment
Straight-on eye-level composition of Youmei presenting an iced bubble tea beside a fresh fruit arrangement.

Foreground & Center:
- [Focal Character/Object]: Youmei (小林友美)—a Japanese student rendered with a pill-shaped torso, neat chin-length dark bob hair helmet with straight fringe, and a warm upward arc smile, wearing her signature open coral-pink cardigan over a crisp white collared blouse, holding a beverage cup at chest height with both hands.
- [Secondary Character/Props]: A tall transparent cylinder cup filled with dark spherical pearls and a thick diagonal straw, a low soft-cornered light wood table, and a wide shallow ceramic bowl displaying a crescent watermelon slice and two bright orange spheres.

Background & Environment:
- The Space: A minimalist classroom lounge with flat horizontal floor planes and clean vertical room partitions.
- Framing & Backdrops: A large circular wall aperture looking out onto the soft undulating silhouette of distant green mountain peaks under an open sky.

Lighting & Visual Balance:
- Warm golden sunlight pouring through the circular aperture onto the fruit bowl, contrasted with cool sage-green interior planes, leading the gaze directly from Youmei's face down to the boba pearls and fruit.

## Lesson 16: 台灣 · Taiwan (B1L16-R03)

Scene Concept: Panorama of the Island
Layered composite perspective connecting a seaside night market stall in the foreground with high-speed rail tracks and soaring mountain peaks behind.

Foreground & Center:
- [Focal Character/Object]: Zhongming (李中明)—a welcoming young host in rolled sleeves and a dark apron standing beside a steaming bamboo food stall, gesturing with a friendly smile toward a freshly plated delicacy.
- [Secondary Character/Props]: A stack of three round cylindrical bamboo steamers billowing puffy steam clouds; a bowl of beef noodles with two diagonal chopsticks; a tall cup of iced bubble tea; and an aerodynamic silver bullet train speeding across a curved viaduct in the midground.

Background & Environment:
- The Space: A dynamic multi-tiered environment: a street-level market apron transitioning into elevated railway arches, sweeping coastal cliffs, and turquoise ocean waters.
- Framing & Backdrops: Twin rounded red paper lanterns framing the upper corners of the scene, with sharp majestic mountain pyramids rising against an azure sea horizon dotted with white triangular sailboat sails.

Lighting & Visual Balance:
- Warm, festive crimson and golden lantern glow illuminating the vendor cart and food steam in the foreground, balanced against the crisp, brilliant midday cyan and emerald hues of the island landscape.
